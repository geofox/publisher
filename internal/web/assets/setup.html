Opening the setup link…

Sign-in needs Publisher's https address in PUBLIC_URL: passkeys and the sign-in cookie only work on https (or on http://localhost).

To get https: tailscale serve on a tailnet, or a reverse proxy such as Caddy or Traefik. Then set PUBLIC_URL to that address, restart, and open the new setup link from the log.
